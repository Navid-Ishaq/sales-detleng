Customer approach

Understand first.
Recommend second.

I would rather make the right recommendation than force the wrong sale. A customer who feels listened to is more likely to trust the conversation.

Listen→Understand→Check→Explain→Recommend→Follow up

“Our broadband is too slow.”

Ask about the household, connected devices, current service and how the internet is used before discussing suitable availability-based options.

“The Wi-Fi is bad upstairs.”

Explore whether the issue is broadband speed or coverage inside the home, then explain the appropriate connectivity solution clearly.

“We want broadband and television together.”

Understand viewing and connectivity needs, check service availability and explain a suitable broadband and TV combination without unnecessary complexity.
